Sky Maker

WindLight skies, water and day cycles for Firestorm, tuned for PBR. Everything runs in your browser.

One water goes with the whole project. It’s saved as water/Default.xml, which the day cycle importer needs, and again under the project name.

Makes Night, Sunrise, Noon and Sunset versions and keys them at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00. Drag keys on the timeline under the preview, or scrub it to see the blend.

    No keyframes yet.

    More than 4 keyframes means setting Reflection Probe Ambiance by hand on each one after import.

    Drop a photo or HDRI .exr, .hdr, JPEG, PNG or WebP. Equirectangular (2:1) images are read as HDRIs.

    Each image makes a new sky, named after the file, with starting values to tune. For a normal photo, place the sun yourself on the Sky tab.
