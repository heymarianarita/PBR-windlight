Editing the sky for

Each key on the day cycle has its own sky. Pick one to edit it.

One water goes with the whole project. It’s saved as water/Default.xml, which the day cycle importer needs, and again under the project name.

Makes Night, Sunrise, Noon and Sunset versions of the sky and keys them at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00. Each key has its own sky. On the timeline under the preview, drag a key to move it, or click anywhere to see the sky at that time.

    No day cycle yet: the look is a single sky.

    Generate from an image

    photo or HDRI
    Drop a photo or HDRI .exr, .hdr, JPEG, PNG or WebP. Equirectangular (2:1) images are read as HDRIs.

    Each image makes a new sky, named after the file, with starting values to tune. It works on the whole look: sky colours, haze, clouds and sun. For a normal photo, place the sun yourself afterwards.
